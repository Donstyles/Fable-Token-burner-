# Prompt card: app icon

**Asset id:** `app-icon`
**Used by:** `npm run icons -- pipeline/inbox/app-icon.png` → PWA and iOS icons
**Output spec:** square, 1024×1024 or larger, PNG, no transparency, no text

## Paste this prompt

> Late-1990s painted fantasy illustration in the spirit of a classic
> first-person party role-playing game. Rich, saturated oil-paint colours on
> warm earth tones: ochre, umber, moss green, slate blue, with gold and deep
> red accents. Strong, simple shapes and clear silhouettes; dramatic but
> readable lighting from a single warm key light; slightly grainy painterly
> texture; no modern digital gloss, no lens flare, no photorealism, no text,
> no watermark, no signature.
>
> Subject: a square app icon. A single ornate gold coin, seen straight on,
> engraved with a stylised flame, resting on dark polished marble with warm
> veins. The flame glows faintly and licks just above the coin's rim. The coin
> fills about 70 percent of the frame and is centred, with the marble
> background extending to all four edges so the image can be cropped to a
> circle or rounded square without losing anything important. Simple,
> readable at thumbnail size, no border, no frame, no lettering.

## Checklist before returning

- Square image, at least 1024 pixels on each side.
- The coin is centred and nothing important is within the outer 10 percent.
- No text anywhere, including on the coin.
