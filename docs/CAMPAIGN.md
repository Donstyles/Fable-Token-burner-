# The campaign: The Doors of the Sea Kings

The owner, 2026-11-04: "It should feel like a deliberate and handcrafted campaign that walks the
player through all the content." This sheet is that walk: the thread, the order a player meets
every region, town, dungeon and quest, the level each is pitched at, and the gates between them.
It is the content document the roadmap asks for before content (docs/ROADMAP.md, "No scope
enters mid-milestone"); DESIGN.md records how each piece is built. Act One is a fifth of MM6:
three regions, eight dungeons, more than eleven quests, thirty-one spells, 138 items and
thirty-nine monster records.

## The premise

Long before Gullhaven, the Sea Kings came over the western sea and raised seven **doors** along
this coast: halls cut into the land and sealed with a warden each, over something they would
not name. The seals are failing. The dead walk up out of the Old Halls onto the headland; the
barrow knights ride out under Harrowmere's downs; the drowned come up through the Mire's reeds.
Lettered obelisks, one in every region, say where the First Door lies and how it is opened:
"WHEN THE SEVEN STAND IN LINE, THE FIRST DOOR IS UNDER THE LAST STAR" (Gullhaven), "THE LAST
STAR IS THE ONE THAT DOES NOT TURN. COUNT THE DOORS FROM THE SEA" (Harrowmere), and the third
in the Mire. The First Door is under the ruin on Gullhaven's headland, where a watchtower was
built over it long after; it opens to the three seals the wardens carry, and behind it the Sea
King waits. Act One ends when he is laid and the score is counted.

## What the campaign needs from the systems

- A monster may **carry** a named thing (`MonsterDef.carries`): a seal, a chart; found on the body.
- **The slain stay slain** (`MapState.slain`): a map remembers whom the party killed and when;
  uniques never come back, the rest return after a month, as MM6's maps refilled on a timer.
- **Doors keyed to a thing** (already: `key` is an item id): the Undercroft's three doors take the
  three seals in turn.
- The **score** counts at the end of the thread (step 11), not after the Old Halls.
- A **campaign test** walks the thread's quest ids in order and checks every gate, item,
  monster, person and place exists (src/game/campaign.test.ts).
