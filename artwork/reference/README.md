# Reference & inspiration

Screenshots collected as visual reference for the look and feel of Fable Token
Burner. Every image here is from **Might and Magic VI: The Mandate of Heaven**
(New World Computing / 3DO, 1998). They are third-party material kept for
reference only – do not ship them, or anything traced from them, as game
assets.

All full-size captures are 3440×1440 ultrawide screenshots; the two files
named by date are smaller grabs from the web / a video.

## UI framing (recurring in every shot)

- Marble pillar frame around the whole screen, with a carved stone header.
- Left: large 3D viewport. Right: minimap under an arched stone window,
  two stained-glass panels, a shelf of four leather books (quest log, notes,
  map, calendar), food and gold counters, and four round gold buttons.
- Bottom: four oval party portraits in gold frames, each with a condition
  light and a green/blue HP/SP bar pair. A thin status strip above them shows
  hover text and prompts ("Select the Item to Buy", "tree").
- Typography: gold blackletter for headings and buttons, serif-italic for
  stats, monospace-ish sans for tooltips.
